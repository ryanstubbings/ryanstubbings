A little about me

Addicted to being creative.

I've been a creative professional for over six years, and it still feels as exciting as the day I walked through the doors of my first gig.

In that time I've looked after well-loved brands with big, passionate audiences, led campaigns from first idea to final file, worked with huge partners like Nike and Emirates on products and co-branded content, and managed a team of designers, externals and freelancers.

I studied graphic design at RMIT and film production at SAE, which is probably why I care as much about the story as the layout. I like clear ideas, tidy systems and a team that loves the craft.
