03 / Melbourne United.

Melbourne United (NBL)

Digital work created as Graphic Designer at Melbourne United Basketball Club in the NBL, from September 2021 to April 2022, covering the club's social channels from game day to the final score.

Visual Design · Digital · Social

The direction

Made for game day.

Digital specs for the club's socials. Game day announcements, quarter-time scores and player stat lines, built as one system so every post reads as United at a glance.

The program

Made for print.

Game Day programs for home games at John Cain Arena. A new cover for every opponent, each built around one player, so the series holds together on the stand and still feels fresh each round.

The round

Made for the big screen.

Key art for Melbourne United's Multicultural Game against the Adelaide 36ers, built to promote ticket sales for the round.
